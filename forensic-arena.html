BAGIAN 1: TEKS TUNGGAL — TEENAGE BRAIN & SLEEP (SOAL 1 – 3)

Exhibit Passage 1 // Health & Biology Level B1–B2

During teenage years, the human brain changes quickly. The brain removes weak and unused brain connections to make thinking faster and more efficient.

At the same time, protective layers grow around nerve wires, helping electrical messages travel up to one hundred times faster across decision-making areas.

However, the emotional part of the brain matures several years earlier than the logical part. Because of this, teenagers often react strongly to peer pressure rather than thinking carefully before acting.

Scientists explain that this difference is not a biological mistake. In ancient times, a desire for excitement helped young humans leave home and learn necessary survival skills.

Nevertheless, chronic lack of sleep and heavy school stress can slow down healthy brain growth, often making it difficult for students to concentrate during class.

BAGIAN 3: DOUBLE TEXT & FAKTA VS OPINI — AI HIRING (SOAL 6 – 8)

TEXT A: THE COMPANY MANAGER PERSPECTIVE

[A1] Automated computer software can read up to 50,000 job applications every hour, cutting hiring time from forty days down to just three days.

[A2] By grading every CV using the exact same rules, computers avoid human problems such as interviewer tiredness, favoritism, and personal prejudice.

[A3] Companies should celebrate AI recruiters as an undeniably perfect system that always guarantees complete fairness for all applicants.

TEXT B: THE FAIRNESS RESEARCHER PERSPECTIVE

[B1] Because AI systems learn from past hiring records, they often repeat old social biases against applicants who took time off to care for their children.

[B2] An independent test found that software lowered applicant scores by up to 28% when resumes mentioned women's colleges or female sports clubs.

[B3] Governments must urgently pass strict laws to inspect AI software before companies use it to reject job seekers.

BAGIAN 4: TEKS MANDIRI — INDOOR VERTICAL FARMING (SOAL 9 – 10)

Exhibit Passage 4 // Agriculture & City Life Level B1–B2

Indoor vertical farming grows fresh vegetables inside tall building towers. In these farms, plant roots hang in the air and receive a spray of nutrient water every three to five minutes.

Agricultural tests show that indoor vertical towers use 95% less freshwater and 90% less land area compared to traditional outdoor soil farms.

City leaders claim that building vertical farms inside empty warehouses is the single most wonderful idea that will completely solve world hunger forever.

However, because the roots hang openly in the air without protective dirt around them, these towers require constant electricity to power the water misting pumps.

An unexpected power blackout lasting just thirty-five minutes dries out the roots very fast, destroying up to 80% of developing vegetables inside the room.

Economists warn that local governments must support backup battery systems before indoor farms can truly survive unexpected electrical failures.

SOAL 09 // TRUE/FALSE MATRIX (PASSAGE 4) 10 Poin

9. Tentukan apakah pernyataan berikut BENAR (TRUE) atau SALAH (FALSE) berdasarkan informasi dalam Exhibit Passage 4:

I. Indoor vertical farming uses 95% less freshwater than traditional soil farming.

II. Because plants are surrounded by thick dirt, indoor crops are completely safe from electrical power outages.

III. A power outage as short as thirty-five minutes can kill up to 80% of crops through rapid root drying.

SOAL 10 // FACT/OPINION MATRIX (PASSAGE 4) 10 Poin

10. Klasifikasikan masing-masing klaim dari Exhibit Passage 4 sebagai FAKTA (FACT) atau OPINI (OPINION):

I. "Hanging plant roots receive a spray of nutrient water every three to five minutes."

II. "Vertical farms inside empty warehouses are the single most wonderful idea that will completely solve world hunger forever."

III. "Local governments must support backup battery systems before indoor farms can truly survive electrical failures."
